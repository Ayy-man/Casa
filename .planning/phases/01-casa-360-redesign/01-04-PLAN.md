<objective>
Build the Vault — the data-discovery surface at `/vault`. A landing page of 8 role-pinned cards drills into table sub-pages, each with a sortable table whose rows open ~500px right side-sheets; the five deep-linkable `[id]` detail routes back the side-sheets. This is the surface that absorbs every legacy data table (`/properties`, `/bookings`, `/cleanings`, `/claims`) into one IA.

Purpose: This is ROADMAP SC3. After this plan a real operator can open `/vault`, see their role-pinned data cards, drill into any of the table sub-pages, open a row's side-sheet, and deep-link a detail record. The legacy-route retirement that *redirects* into these pages is plan 05's job — this plan builds the destinations.
Output: New `casa/vault-card.tsx`; the `/vault` landing; 8 sub-pages (`properties`, `bookings`, `cleanings`, `claims`, `owners`, `pipeline`, `compliance` tables + `financials` summary); 5 `[id]` detail routes.
</objective>
